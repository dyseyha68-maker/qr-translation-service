# Project Requirements Specification

## 1. Project Background & Context
A translation and document legalization service company located in Phnom Penh, Cambodia works closely with local transportation providers—primarily taxi, PassApp, and tuk-tuk drivers. These drivers frequently transport foreign visitors, expatriates, and local residents seeking certified translations (legal documents, visa paperwork, certificates, business agreements, medical documents).

To incentivize drivers to recommend the company's services, the company provides drivers with unique, branded QR code cards/stickers. When a passenger scans the QR code, they are directed straight into the company's official Telegram bot with the driver's unique referral identifier attached. Once the customer places an order and pays, the referring driver earns an earned referral commission.

---

## 2. Core Business Problem & Objectives

### The Problem
- Previously, driver referrals were handled informally (verbal recommendations, paper slips, or manual Telegram messages).
- Staff frequently lost track of which driver referred which customer.
- Commission payouts were disputed or delayed due to lack of verifiable proof.
- Drivers lost trust, reducing referral volume.

### The Objectives
1. **Verifiable Attribution:** Unambiguously track each customer from the physical QR code scan to the driver who holds that code.
2. **Streamlined Intake:** Automatically turn Telegram referrals into actionable leads inside an intuitive staff portal.
3. **Transparent Order & Payment Lifecycle:** Connect the customer lead directly to the translation order and its verified payment.
4. **Automated & Fair Commission Ledger:** Automatically compute commissions upon payment confirmation, maintain an auditable ledger, and track disbursements (cash or Bakong / KHQR).

---

## 3. Key Stakeholders & Personas

### 1. Driver / Referrer (Taxi / Tuk-Tuk)
- Receives physical printed QR cards/stickers generated by the system.
- Has a registered profile (Name, Phone number, Vehicle number / type, ABA Bank / Bakong KHQR account).
- Earns a fixed or percentage commission when their referred passenger pays for translation services.
- Receives commission payouts from the company cashier.

### 2. Customer / Passenger
- Scans the driver's QR code in the vehicle using smartphone camera.
- Automatically redirected to the company's Telegram bot with `start=REF_{CODE}`.
- Initiates contact with the translation company easily.

### 3. Front Desk / Translation Staff
- Monitors the staff portal for new incoming Telegram leads.
- Contacts customer via Telegram / phone, collects documents, provides quotes.
- Creates translation orders with service details, source/target languages, price, and deadline.

### 4. Cashier / Finance Officer
- Verifies customer payment slips (Cash, ABA Bank, KHQR/Bakong).
- Reviews generated commission records.
- Disburses commission payments to drivers and uploads/attaches disbursement proofs.

### 5. Super Admin / General Manager
- Oversees overall business performance (top referrers, total orders, commission payouts).
- Manages staff accounts, roles, commission rate policies, and system settings.

## 4. End-to-End Business Workflow

```text
[Step 1: Driver Onboarding]
   Staff registers Driver -> System generates unique Referral Code & printable QR code.

[Step 2: Customer Scans QR]
   Passenger scans QR -> Smartphone launches Telegram: https://t.me/<Bot>?start=REF_XXXXXX

[Step 3: Telegram Bot Processing]
   Bot intercepts /start payload -> Resolves Driver -> Records Lead & Attribution Snapshot ->
   Sends welcoming message to Customer & Dispatches internal notification to Staff.

[Step 4: Lead Handling]
   Staff views Lead on portal -> Reviews customer details -> Communicates with Customer.

[Step 5: Order Creation]
   Customer confirms translation -> Staff converts Lead to Order (Price, Language, Pages, Deadline).

[Step 6: Customer Payment]
   Customer pays invoice (Cash / ABA / Bakong) -> Finance verifies and marks Payment as Paid.

[Step 7: Commission Generation]
   Payment confirmation triggers Commission engine -> Calculates commission amount ->
   Creates pending Commission record linked to Driver & Order.

[Step 8: Driver Payout]
   Finance / Cashier approves commission -> Pays Driver (via Bakong or Cash) ->
   Attaches payment slip -> Marks Commission as Disbursed.
```

---

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Data Integrity:** Strict foreign key constraints and transactional consistency across financial tables.
- **Auditing:** Every lead attribution, status change, and payment verification must be logged with timestamps and user identifiers.
- **Performance:** Instantaneous lead creation upon bot webhook hit (< 500ms).
- **Usability:** High-contrast, clean web interface tailored for busy counter staff on desktop and tablet screens.
- **Localization & Currency:** Native support for both USD ($) and Cambodian Riel (KHR) transactions.
